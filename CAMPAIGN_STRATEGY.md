# CAMPAIGN_STRATEGY.md

## Product/Service Overview and Unique Value Proposition
NutriTrack is an AI nutrition coaching SaaS for busy professionals, offering AI meal planning, calorie/macro tracking, grocery list generation, weekly nutrition reports, and email coaching nudges. Its unique value proposition is AI-driven personalization, comprehensive features (planning, tracking, lists, reports, nudges), and being tailored for busy professionals' lifestyles, solving their lack of time and need for personalized guidance.

## Target Audience Personas

### Persona 1: Ambitious Anna
*   **Age:** 32
*   **Job:** Marketing Manager, Tech Startup
*   **Pain Points:** Struggles to find time for healthy meal prep due to demanding work schedule. Often resorts to unhealthy takeout. Wants to improve energy levels and fitness but lacks consistent guidance.
*   **Online Behavior:** Active on LinkedIn for professional networking, browses Instagram for quick healthy recipes and fitness inspiration, occasionally checks Reddit for productivity hacks and health tips.

### Persona 2: Driven David
*   **Age:** 38
*   **Job:** Senior Software Engineer
*   **Pain Points:** Health-conscious but overwhelmed by manual calorie/macro tracking. Wants data-driven insights and personalized plans without the hassle. Travels frequently for work, making consistent healthy eating difficult.
*   **Online Behavior:** Reads tech blogs and industry news, uses Twitter for quick updates, searches for efficient solutions on Google, might be in health/fitness subreddits.

## Competitor Analysis
*   **Noom:** Focuses on psychology-based weight loss. Content often emphasizes behavioral change and habit formation.
*   **Simple:** Offers science-based tools for tracking and healthy living. Marketing highlights ease of use and personalized plans.
*   **Lasta: Your Wellness Coach:** AI-created, focuses on weight loss, muscle building, general health. Simplifies calorie tracking via chat, personalized meal planning, daily accountability.
*   **Sortedfood:** Offers recipes, meal planning, and community. Content focuses on easy, tasty, and filling recipes.
*   **General Observations:** Competitors emphasize user-friendly interfaces, ease of use, comprehensive features (tracking, recipes, workouts, education), personalized plans, and supportive communities.

## Platform Strategy
*   **LinkedIn:** Professional audience, ideal for B2B-like content (productivity, health for professionals), thought leadership.
*   **Instagram:** Visual platform, great for meal prep ideas, healthy recipes, progress visuals, short tips.
*   **Reddit (specific subreddits like r/mealprep, r/health, r/productivity):** Engaged communities, good for direct interaction, answering questions, sharing value, subtle product mentions.
*   **Email Marketing:** Direct communication for coaching nudges, weekly reports, early adopter program updates.

## Messaging Pillars
1.  **Time-Saving & Efficiency:** Highlight how NutriTrack automates planning and tracking, freeing up time for busy professionals.
2.  **Personalized & Data-Driven:** Emphasize AI's ability to create tailored plans and provide actionable insights.
3.  **Sustainable Healthy Habits:** Focus on long-term health and consistency, not just quick fixes.
4.  **Effortless Nutrition:** Position NutriTrack as the easy solution for complex nutrition challenges.

## Content Types Per Platform
*   **LinkedIn:**
    *   Text posts: Articles on "AI for busy professional nutrition," "Time-saving meal prep strategies."
    *   Carousels: "5 Ways AI Streamlines Your Nutrition."
    *   Short videos: Testimonials from busy professionals.
*   **Instagram:**
    *   Reels/Stories: Quick healthy meal prep demos, "Day in the life of a NutriTrack user," "Nutrition hacks for busy schedules."
    *   Carousels: "Before & After" (progress), "Grocery List Essentials."
    *   Image posts: Visually appealing healthy meals, infographics on macro tracking.
*   **Reddit:**
    *   Text posts: Participate in discussions, offer value (e.g., "Ask Me Anything: AI Nutrition Expert"), share helpful tips (without direct selling initially).
    *   Links: Share blog posts or helpful resources.
*   **Email:**
    *   Newsletters: Weekly nutrition reports, healthy recipes, coaching nudges, product updates.
    *   Onboarding series: Guide new users through features.

## Posting Cadence
*   **LinkedIn:** 2-3 times/week
*   **Instagram:** 4-5 times/week (mix of posts, stories, reels)
*   **Reddit:** Daily engagement in relevant subreddits, 1-2 value-add posts/week.
*   **Email:** Weekly newsletter, triggered emails for onboarding/nudges.

## Tone of Voice Guidelines
*   **Professional:** Credible, expert, authoritative.
*   **Encouraging:** Supportive, motivating, positive.
*   **Data-Driven:** Factual, evidence-based, results-oriented.
*   **Efficient:** Concise, clear, respectful of time.
*   **Do:** Use clear, actionable language. Focus on benefits and solutions. Highlight AI's intelligence.
*   **Don't:** Use overly casual slang. Make unsubstantiated claims. Overwhelm with jargon. Sound preachy or judgmental.
