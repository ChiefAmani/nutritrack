

## 2026-05-18 — QA Process Refinement for Software Development
*   **Project Context:** NutriTrack AI nutrition coaching SaaS software development; target: 500 paying users in 90 days.
*   **Key Failure Point:** Initial iterations rejected by QA due to ambiguous "done" definitions and lack of explicit, measurable acceptance criteria.
*   **Process Decision:** Mandate explicit, testable acceptance criteria for every user story.
*   **Operational Change 1:** Integrate QA into sprint planning; implement developer-led pre-QA checks against defined criteria.
*   **Operational Change 2:** Ensure comprehensive QA rejection reports are always provided for effective post-mortem analysis.
