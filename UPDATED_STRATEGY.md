# Updated Strategy: Integrating User Research with MVP Development

The `RESEARCH_BRIEF.md` provides a strong foundation for understanding our target users. To effectively leverage this research for our MVP development and achieve our aggressive user acquisition goals, we need to integrate the research findings directly into our technical roadmap.

## Next Steps:

1.  **Execute User Interviews (CSO/Product Lead):** The CSO/Product Lead should immediately initiate and complete the 10-15 qualitative interviews as outlined in `RESEARCH_BRIEF.md`. This is critical for validating our core MVP features and understanding user pain points.
2.  **Synthesize Research Findings (CSO/Product Lead):** Upon completion of interviews, the CSO/Product Lead must synthesize the findings into a concise "User Research Summary Report." This report should highlight:
    *   Top 3-5 validated pain points.
    *   Most desired MVP features (from the list in `RESEARCH_BRIEF.md`).
    *   Critical feedback on proposed features.
    *   Insights on preferred onboarding and pricing.
3.  **Technical Prioritization Workshop (CTO, Product Lead, Lead Engineer):** Immediately after the "User Research Summary Report" is available, I will lead a workshop with the Product Lead and Lead Engineer. The goal is to:
    *   Review the research findings.
    *   Prioritize backend features for the MVP based on user feedback and business impact.
    *   Refine user stories and acceptance criteria for the current sprint.
    *   Identify any technical risks or opportunities highlighted by user feedback.
4.  **MVP Backend Development (CTO/Engineering Team):** The engineering team will proceed with developing the prioritized core MVP backend features. This development will be directly informed by the user research, ensuring we build what users truly need and value.
    *   Focus on scalable architecture for AI meal planning, calorie/macro tracking, and grocery list generation.
    *   Implement robust APIs for data exchange.
    *   Ensure security and data privacy are paramount.
5.  **Iterative Feedback Loop:** Establish a continuous feedback loop where early MVP users can provide input, which will then inform subsequent development sprints.

This strategy ensures that our technical development is not just feature-driven, but deeply user-centric, directly addressing the pain points and needs of our target professionals, thereby increasing our chances of achieving 500 paying users and $4500 MRR.
