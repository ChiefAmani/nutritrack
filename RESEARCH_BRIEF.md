# RESEARCH_BRIEF.md

## Project: Conduct Target Professional User Interviews
## Archetype: RESEARCH PROJECT

## Key Questions:
1.  What are the primary pain points busy professionals face regarding nutrition and meal planning?
2.  How do current solutions (or lack thereof) address these pain points, and where do they fall short?
3.  What are the most critical features users would expect from an AI nutrition coaching SaaS?
4.  How valuable is AI-driven personalization, calorie/macro tracking, grocery list generation, weekly reports, and email nudges to them?
5.  What are their expectations for onboarding complexity and data entry effort?
6.  What pricing models would be most appealing and perceived as fair?
7.  What specific feedback do they have on the proposed core MVP features?

## Sources to Check:
-   Primary: Direct interviews with target urban professionals (25-45, busy, health-conscious).
-   Secondary: Existing market research on health & wellness tech, competitor reviews (if applicable).

## Methodology:
-   Qualitative interviews (1:1, semi-structured).
-   Target 10-15 participants fitting the ideal customer persona.
-   Interviews conducted remotely via video call, approximately 30-45 minutes each.
-   Record and transcribe interviews (with consent).

## Target Participants:
-   Urban professionals, aged 25-45.
-   Individuals who identify as busy and health-conscious.
-   Currently struggling with meal planning, nutrition, or maintaining healthy habits due to time constraints.
-   Open to using technology/AI for personal wellness.

## Analysis Framework:
-   Thematic analysis of interview transcripts to identify recurring pain points, needs, feature preferences, and usability concerns.
-   Categorize feedback by core MVP features.
-   Prioritize insights based on frequency and intensity of user sentiment.

## Deliverable:
-   RESEARCH_BRIEF.md (this document)
-   Summary report of interview findings, including key insights, validated assumptions, and areas for improvement for the MVP.

## Success Criteria:
-   Clear understanding of target user pain points and unmet needs.
-   Validation or invalidation of core MVP features and value proposition.
-   Actionable insights for product development and refinement.
-   Identification of preferred pricing structures and onboarding experiences.
